# Problem Statement

Social media teams often struggle to identify which content types, platforms, and posting times deliver the strongest engagement. Without a structured analysis, resources may be allocated to ineffective posts and publishing windows. This project addresses that challenge by analyzing historical social media performance data, isolating the drivers of engagement, and translating the findings into practical marketing actions.

The business question is: Which social media posts and posting times generate the highest engagement, and what actions can marketers take to improve performance?

The project uses a reproducible data pipeline, SQL analysis, exploratory analysis, and a Streamlit dashboard to study performance patterns and support evidence-based marketing decisions.
